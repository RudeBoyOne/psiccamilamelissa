# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Adults in São Paulo seeking psychotherapy or psychoanalysis. They're likely feeling stuck, anxious, or curious about self-understanding. They're thoughtful enough to seek depth — not a quick fix. Many are first-time therapy seekers; the site needs to make psychoanalysis feel accessible without dumbing it down.

## Product Purpose

Establish Camila Melissa de Souza as a credible, approachable psychoanalyst in São Paulo. The site exists to:
- Communicate her clinical approach (psychoanalysis, Freudian)
- Demonstrate credibility through credentials, published articles, and experience
- Make it easy for potential patients to initiate contact via WhatsApp or email
- Share published academic articles via the built-in PDF viewer

Success is a visitor who feels "this is the right professional for me" and reaches out.

## Brand Personality

Intelectual, introspectiva, elegante. The site must feel like a thoughtful, well-appointed consulting room — serious but not cold, intellectual but not pretentious, warm without being saccharine. The existing dark purple + gold palette already captures this tension between depth and warmth.

## Anti-references

- **Generic psychology sites** — stock photos of smiling people, pastel blues, clichéd brain/hand icons, "transforme sua vida" banners. This is the cardinal sin.
- **Cold clinical/medical** — sterile white, hospital-adjacent, impersonal. Psychoanalysis is about human depth, not diagnosis codes.
- **Too informal/juvenile** — playful colors, trendy UI patterns, casual tone. Undermines professional authority for a serious service.

## Design Principles

1. **Authority without coldness.** Clinical credibility must never feel sterile. The dark purple foundation carries warmth; typography and gold accents carry refinement.

2. **Depth made visible.** Psychoanalysis is about looking inward. The design should feel reflective, not flashy. Space, rhythm, and restraint are features, not absences.

3. **Every pixel is a credential.** A therapist's website is the first impression of their professionalism. No broken layouts, no rough edges, no generic templates.

4. **Warmth through substance.** The warmth comes from intellectual honesty and genuine care — not decorative flourishes, not stock smiles, not "acolhedor" labels. Let the copy, the credentials, and the professional confidence carry the human connection.

5. **Break the psychology-site mold.** No stock photos, no light blue, no clip-art brains, no clichés. The dark purple + gold is already a departure. Double down on what makes this site itself.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard good practices (WCAG AA implied). Portuguese-first; Brazilian cultural context throughout.
